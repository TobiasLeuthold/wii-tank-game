1 player Mouse

WASD drive · mouse aims the turret · Left click shoot · Space drop a mine

  • With 2 players, shots go where your tank faces. Tap left/right to turn just a little for long-range aim; hold to turn faster.
  • Shots bounce off walls once, then pop. The dotted aim line (Options) shows the path.
  • Each tank can have 5 shots and 2 mines out at once. Shots that hit each other both pop.
  • A mine goes off after 10 s, when an enemy drives close, or when it gets shot. The blast also breaks cork blocks.
  • Destroy every enemy tank to clear the mission. If all players are destroyed, you lose a life and retry. Every 5 missions cleared gives you an extra life.
  • Esc / P pause · M music on/off. Progress saves automatically.
